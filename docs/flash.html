⛵ NauticPinnace

Flash the NauticPinnace firmware straight from the browser — no toolchain needed.

Requirements: Chrome or Edge (WebSerial), one of the three supported Waveshare boards connected via USB-C. Flashing erases the on-device configuration. Read the safety notes in the README first — this is uncertified DIY firmware, use at your own risk.
Pick your board carefully. All three are ESP32-S3 and look identical over USB, so the browser cannot choose for you. The wrong firmware leaves the screen dark, because its panel driver does not match the hardware. That is not fatal — flashing the right one fixes it — but there is no warning before it happens.

1. Plug in the display via USB-C.
2. Click the button for your board, pick the USB Serial Device port, wait about a minute.

4-inch
ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-4 Rev 4 · 480 × 480
7-inch
ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-7B · 1024 × 600
5-inch
ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-5B · 1024 × 600

If no port shows up: a board in a crashed state may need a manual bootloader entry — hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT (buttons on the left edge). The 7-inch additionally needs the WCH CH343 driver: it talks through a USB bridge rather than the ESP32's own USB, and without the driver Windows gives it no port at all. The 4-inch and 5-inch need nothing.

After flashing, the display boots in English, asks for your language and shows a demo of all screens. Full setup instructions are in the README.
