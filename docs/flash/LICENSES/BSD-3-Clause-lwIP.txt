Two linked lwIP files have no copyright line of their own but carry the same
conditions and disclaimer: api/netdb.c and netif/ppp/pppos.c (both 7B / 5B
only).

ipv4/igmp.c (all boards) has its own wording, which differs in clause 3 and
in the disclaimer: it is reproduced in section 2.

The other notices of the lwIP PPP files that the 7B / 5B link are in
BSD-lwIP-PPP.txt.

Sources: ESP-IDF 5.5.5 (components/lwip) for the 7B / 5B; ESP-IDF 4.4.7 with
espressif/esp-lwip a45be9e438f6cf9c54ec150581819c3b95d5af6b for the 4-inch.
COPYING and the headers read the same in both, except that dhcp6.c is not
linked on the 4-inch.
