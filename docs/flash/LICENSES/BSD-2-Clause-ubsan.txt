ubsan.c of ESP-IDF - licence text
=================================

ESP-IDF's components/esp_system/ubsan.c (undefined-behaviour-sanitizer
handlers) is linked on all boards (ESP-IDF 4.4.7 on the 4-inch, 5.5.5 on the
7B / 5B; the notice reads the same in both). Only its empty hook function
__ubsan_include (5 bytes) is placed in the image; the linker discards the
handlers themselves.
